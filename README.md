# UNP_PROJECT_BI_WITH_POWER_BI_COFFEE_SALES_ANALYSIS

I worked on this project in collaboration with UNP, where the primary objective was to analyze coffee sales data and derive meaningful business insights using Power BI. The project focused on understanding overall sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product trends within a coffee retail environment.

The dataset included key attributes such as product types, sales revenue, order volume, time of purchase, and monthly as well as location-based patterns. Using Power BI, I transformed raw and unstructured data into interactive dashboards that allow users to easily explore and interpret the data. Through effective data modeling and visualization techniques, the dashboard provides a clear and comprehensive view of business performance.

One of the main aspects of the analysis was identifying key performance indicators (KPIs), including total revenue (112K), total orders (3547), peak hour revenue, and peak hour order volume. These KPIs helped in evaluating the overall performance of the business and identifying critical areas for improvement.

I created various visualizations such as bar charts, line graphs, and slicers to represent product-wise revenue contribution, monthly sales trends, quarterly performance, and order distribution across different times of the day. The analysis revealed that afternoon and night periods generate higher sales compared to mornings, highlighting an opportunity to improve early-hour performance.

The project also provided insights into product performance, where items like Latte, Cappuccino, and Americano contributed significantly to revenue, while others required strategic improvements. Additionally, time-based analysis helped identify peak hours (around 10 AM), which is crucial for managing operations and staffing efficiently.

Another important outcome was identifying monthly trends, where certain months like April showed lower revenue, indicating seasonal fluctuations in demand. Based on these insights, practical business solutions were suggested, such as introducing morning offers, combo deals, promotional strategies, and improving customer engagement through loyalty programs.

Overall, this project demonstrates how Power BI can be effectively used to convert raw data into actionable insights. It highlights the importance of data-driven decision-making in improving sales strategies, optimizing operations, enhancing customer satisfaction, and driving business growth.
